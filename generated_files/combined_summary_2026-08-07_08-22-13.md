The AI landscape is rapidly evolving, driven by an accelerating trend of open-sourcing foundational AI infrastructure and models. This shift is shrinking the competitive "moat" around proprietary AI, moving the focus from who has the best model to who builds the best product. Developers are increasingly leveraging persistent, autonomous AI agents capable of handling complex, multi-step tasks across various domains.
